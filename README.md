# AnimusLab Case Studies

A public archive of case studies, governance frameworks, architectural analyses, and implementation evidence produced by AnimusLab.

This repository documents practical approaches to AI governance, policy enforcement, auditability, observability, and institutional trust for advanced AI systems.

The goal is to provide transparent, reproducible examples that demonstrate how governance principles can be translated into technical infrastructure.

## Areas of Research

- AI Governance
- Runtime Policy Enforcement
- Auditability & Decision Provenance
- Behavioral Intelligence
- Agent Oversight
- Observability Systems
- Institutional AI Infrastructure

## Projects

### Anchor
Governance infrastructure for deterministic policy enforcement, auditability, and decision provenance in agentic systems.

### ShadowWatch
Behavioral intelligence and anomaly detection research focused on identifying governance, security, and operational risks.

### Governance Assets
Checklists, frameworks, reference architectures, and implementation guides for institutions adopting advanced AI systems.
